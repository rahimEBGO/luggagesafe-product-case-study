01 / The problem

A two-sided trust challenge.

Travellers value convenient, reliable storage close to where they are going. Local partners need a clear process for receiving, tracking and returning bags. The product challenge is to make both sides of the exchange feel straightforward and trustworthy.

01

Traveller confidence

Make it easy to understand the booking, drop-off and collection journey before committing.

02

Partner usability

Give local storage partners a practical workflow for handling reservations and luggage hand-offs.

03

Operational trust

Design access controls and traceable workflows to support accountable storage operations.

02 / Product approach

Build the core journey first.

As founder and product lead, I have been shaping the product and coordinating AI-assisted implementation, with a focus on connecting customer booking, partner operations and administration in one service.

Core experience in the build

  • ✓Customer booking flow and payment integration in a test environment.
  • ✓Separate customer, partner and admin workflows.
  • ✓QR-based access and verification flows under security review.
  • ✓Booking notifications and operational hand-off considerations.

Current stage & constraints

The product is nearly built and in testing and launch preparation. A successful test flow is not the same as validated live-market performance.

Approach: prioritise end-to-end reliability, safe access control and partner usability before scaling demand or making performance claims.

03 / Demand growth

What I would test after launch.

The growth plan should start with measurable experiments rather than assumptions. These are proposed next steps, not experiments or results claimed as completed.

A

Improve search-to-booking

Instrument the funnel from location search to completed booking; identify drop-off points and test clearer availability, trust information and checkout steps.

B

Activate supply locally

Measure partner onboarding, time to first booking and location coverage. Focus demand campaigns where usable storage supply is available.

C

Build repeat demand

Track repeat bookings and customer feedback; test post-booking communication and targeted re-engagement only after a reliable baseline exists.

04 / Reflection

Product thinking under constraints.

Building with limited resources has made sequencing especially important: connect the core journey, test real user and partner workflows, review security, and establish trustworthy metrics before scaling.

What this project demonstrates

  • ↗End-to-end ownership of a marketplace product direction.
  • ↗Thinking across customer experience and partner operations.
  • ↗Hands-on AI-assisted product development and iterative testing.

What comes next
